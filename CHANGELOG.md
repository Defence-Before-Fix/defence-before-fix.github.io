# Changelog

Two documents, versioned independently. A toolchain clause can be added without reissuing the
method.

## Method specification (SPEC.md)

### 1.0.0, unpublished

First normative version. Six clauses in section 3, decision rights in section 4, conformance in
section 7, operation under AI-assisted development in section 8. Hardened by eleven cold reads,
three adversarial critique rounds and three execution tests on real defects in php-qa-ci and
ts-qa-ci before publication.

## Toolchain specification (TOOLING-SPEC.md)

### 0.1.0, draft

First draft. States what a toolchain must offer so that a practitioner can follow the method:
custom rules, a single-rule harness, identifier resolution, a derived listing of active defences,
the project record, self-application, and the conformance definition in section 11.
