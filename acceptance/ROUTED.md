# Passages routed out of a cohort

A finding is a passage two or more readers of one cohort named as confusing under question 4.
`ACCEPTANCE.md` routes here a finding that lies outside the text the change being tested wrote
or altered, rather than blocking the change whose cohort raised it, so that each change is
judged on what it changed and the document is still made clearer, one change at a time. The
editor reads this list before cutting a release.

A finding is open until a change that reworks its passage passes a cohort, when the line names
that run and moves below the rule, or until the editor closes it with a reason recorded here. A
finding raised again by a later run is noted on its line, not listed twice. The status column
holds `open`, or the `editor/<slug>` branch or pull request number of the change that is
reworking the passage. The rows raised by runs 31 to 52 were raised under the earlier rules, on
untouched text of the change those runs tested, and are listed here so that they are not lost.
